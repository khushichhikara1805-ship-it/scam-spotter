💡 Online safety tips

Simple habits that stop most scams.

🔒

Never share OTP or PIN

No bank or company will ever ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV or password.

🔗

Don't click unknown links

Open the official website or app yourself instead of tapping a link.

⏰

Beware of urgency

"Act now" or "account will be blocked" is a pressure tactic. Pause and verify.

🎁

No free prizes

You cannot win a contest you never entered. Prize and lottery messages are scams.

💼

Real jobs are free

Genuine employers never charge a registration or training fee.

📞

Report it

Visit cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline 1930 (India), quickly if money was lost.
